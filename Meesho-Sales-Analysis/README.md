# Meesho Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes a practice e-commerce dataset using Microsoft Power BI. It focuses on understanding sales performance and presenting business insights through an interactive dashboard.

## Problem Statement
The objective is to analyze e-commerce sales data to understand overall sales performance, category-wise trends, geographical performance, and profitability wherever the required fields are available.

## Dataset Information
- Dataset type: Practice dataset
- Number of rows: 26,000
- File formats: CSV and Excel
- Analysis and visualization: Power BI
- Calculations: DAX
- Data cleaning: Multiple tools

## Project Objectives
- Analyze sales performance.
- Compare available product categories.
- Evaluate geographical performance where applicable.
- Present key performance indicators.
- Build an interactive dashboard.
- Identify business insights from the actual analysis.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Overall Performance
- Total Sales: ₹8.47M
- Total Profit: ₹2.28M
- Total Quantity Sold: 43K
- Total Orders: Approximately 26K
- Average Order Value: 325.64

### 2. Category Performance
- Fashion is the highest-selling category, with sales of approximately 2.55M.
- Home & Kitchen is the second-highest category, with sales of approximately 1.85M.
- Beauty contributes approximately 1.25M in sales.
- Grocery has the lowest sales among the six displayed categories.

### 3. Product Performance
- The Product Performance dashboard compares profit across six product categories.
- Fashion has the highest displayed category profit, at approximately 0.69M.
- Grocery has the lowest displayed category profit, at approximately 0.18M.

### 4. Geographical Performance
- The Sales Dashboard compares sales across states.
- Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan appear among the leading states in the displayed chart.
- State-level performance can be explored further using the dashboard filters.

### 5. Order and Payment Analysis
- The dashboard compares orders by payment mode.
- UPI has the highest displayed count, followed by Cash on Delivery.
- Credit Card, Debit Card, and Wallet have lower displayed counts than UPI.

### 6. Monthly Sales Trend
- The monthly sales chart shows higher sales during the early months of the displayed period.
- Sales decline noticeably around September and remain lower toward the end of the displayed year.

### 7. Conclusion
The dashboards provide an overview of sales, profitability, category performance, geographical distribution, monthly trends, and payment preferences. These insights can help identify areas for further investigation and support data-driven business decisions.
